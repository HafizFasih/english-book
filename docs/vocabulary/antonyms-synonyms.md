# Antonyms & Synonyms

A rich vocabulary comes not just from knowing words but from knowing **alternatives** — words that mean the same thing (synonyms) and words that mean the opposite (antonyms). These skills are tested in reading comprehension, writing, and vocabulary tasks.

---

## Synonyms

A **synonym** is a word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. Synonyms allow you to vary your language and avoid repetition.

> *Note:* No two words are perfectly identical in meaning — synonyms often have slight differences in tone, formality, or usage. Always choose the most appropriate synonym for the context.

## Antonyms

An **antonym** is a word that has the opposite meaning to another word.

There are three types of antonyms:

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Gradable antonyms** | Opposite ends of a scale; degrees exist in between | hot ↔ cold, fast ↔ slow |
| **Complementary antonyms** | Absolute opposites; no middle ground | alive ↔ dead, true ↔ false |
| **Relational antonyms** | Each word defines the other | teacher ↔ student, employer ↔ employee |
