# Chapter 5 — Email Writing

**Electronic Mail (email)** is a system of electronic correspondence that allows users to send and receive messages over a network of computers and telecommunication links. A message may consist of short notes, extensive text, graphics, images, video clips, or sound.

Email acts as an *electronic post office* — you can send a message at any time, and the recipient reads it at their convenience. The sender and receiver do not need to be online at the same time.

---

## 5.1 Advantages of Email

- Permits sending and receiving messages from anyone with an email address
- Transmits messages almost immediately — extremely fast
- Does not require the receiver to be present; the message goes directly to their inbox
- Reaches the individual's mailbox directly and privately
- Provides a higher degree of secrecy compared to postal mail
- Very cost-effective — can replace hard-copy letters and memos
- Can be sent at any time, eliminating time-zone problems
- Identical messages can be sent to multiple recipients simultaneously

---

## 5.2 Disadvantages of Email

- Not fully confidential — emails can be forwarded or intercepted
- Does not communicate the sender's emotions or tone well
- May be ignored, delayed, or lost in spam filters

---

## 5.3 Email Etiquette

Good email etiquette ensures your message is received and respected:

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Respond promptly** | Reply to messages in a timely manner |
| **Clear subject lines** | Write a subject that summarises the message |
| **Short paragraphs** | Keep paragraphs brief to hold the reader's attention |
| **Be concise** | Avoid rambling; say what you mean directly |
| **Use proper case** | Use upper and lower case — ALL CAPITALS reads as shouting |
| **Avoid inappropriate language** | Maintain a professional and respectful tone |
| **Limit attachments** | Don't add unnecessary files |
| **Use your name** | Include your personal name for identification |
| **Proofread** | Reread the message before sending |
| **Use spell check** | Ensure spelling and grammar are correct |

---

## 5.4 Structure of an Email

### Prefatory Elements

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **To** | The primary recipient's email address |
| **Cc** (Carbon Copy) | Others who should receive a copy |
| **Bcc** (Blind Carbon Copy) | Recipients hidden from all others |
| **Subject** | A brief, clear description of the email's content |
| **Attachments** | Files attached to the message |
| **Greeting** | Begin with the recipient's name or a salutation |

### Formality Levels

Choose the appropriate tone based on your audience and relationship:

| Level | When to Use | Example Greeting |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Formal** | Professors, managers, clients | *Dear Dr. Ahmed,* |
| **Informal** | Colleagues, classmates you know well | *Hi Sarah,* |
| **Casual** | Close friends | *Hey!* |

### Key Principles

- **Conciseness** — get to the point quickly
- **Clarity** — say exactly what you mean
- **Correctness** — accurate grammar and spelling
- **Courtesy** — respectful, professional language

## 5.7 Email Writing Format

```
To: recipient@example.com
Cc: (if applicable)
Subject: [Clear, specific subject line]

Dear [Title and Name],

[Opening paragraph — state your purpose clearly]

[Middle paragraph(s) — provide necessary details]

[Closing paragraph — state any required action or express thanks]

Regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Roll Number / Designation]
[Institution / Organization]
```

## Practice Task

You are a student who has missed several classes due to illness. Write a formal email to your teacher:

- **Recipient:** Mr./Ms./Mrs. [Teacher's Name]
- **Subject:** Missed Classes and Request for Assignments — [Your Name & Roll Number]
- **Goal:** Apologise for your absence, show responsibility, and request support to catch up on missed work.
- **Instructor's email (for reference):** mahakamal@cloud.neduet.edu.pk

Your email should be:
- Formally written
- Concise (no more than three paragraphs)
- Free of spelling and grammar errors
- Courteous and professional in tone
